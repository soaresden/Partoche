Student In 5 steps

You need an Android tablet and a pCloud account (free). The app guides you step by step (the app itself is in French; translations are given in brackets).

  1. Install the app

    Download Partoche And Prof with ⬇️ this direct link (Partoche.apk) and open the file on the tablet (allow “unknown sources” if Android asks). On first launch, choose Élève (Student).

  2. Choose your Partoche folder

    The app offers 3 choices: 🆕 Nouvelle installation (New installation: it creates the folder on your tablet), 📂 J'ai déjà un dossier Partoche (I already have a Partoche folder: it finds it and detects what it contains), or ☁️ Directement dans mon pCloud (Directly in my pCloud — recommended: sign in through the pCloud site, Google and two-factor included). It sets everything up there:

    Partoche/ ├── MSCZ/ 🎼 your scores ├── MesNotes/ ✍️ your annotations, one per score ├── Settings/ ⚙️ your settings, calendar, practice time └── Prof/ 📬 what your teacher sends you
    ☁️ Folder on the tablet? It must be synced with your pCloud (e.g. with the AutoSync app), otherwise your teacher sees nothing. Keep it synced both ways.
    🔑 Directly in pCloud? Nothing to sync: sign in with your pCloud email and password. Google, Apple or Facebook account? First add a password to your pCloud account (my.pcloud.com → Settings → Account → Password), then sign in with your email. Two-factor authentication works: the app asks for the code.

  3. Fill your library

    You choose your pieces: copy your .mscz files into MSCZ. They show up in the app (⟳ Actualiser (Refresh) button).

    💻 From the computer: drag them into pCloud📱 From the tablet: pCloud app → Upload🗂️ Subfolders? No problem🎁 Your teacher can also send you some (tag Nouveau (New))
    🎼 Where do .mscz files come from? Tap ＋ Autres partitions (More scores): the app points you to LibreScore (free, for PC, Mac or Android) to search for a piece and download it as .mscz. Or from MuseScore (File → Save as). Respect the rights of the scores you use.

  4. Share with your teacher

    On pcloud.com: on Partoche, Share → Share link with a password; on Prof, the three dots → Request files (that's the drop box, no account needed for your teacher).

    Then in the app: Options → Partager avec mon prof (Options → Share with my teacher), paste both links: it checks them and prepares the message to send (WhatsApp, email…). When connected directly to pCloud, it even creates the links for you.

    The message contains an invitation link: your teacher clicks it and you appear in their list, nothing to copy.

    In Options → Mon emoji et ma couleur (Options → My emoji and my colour), choose how your teacher sees you.

    💡 Your teacher can't break anything: they read your scores, and only drop files into Prof. They don't need a pCloud account. You're notified of every new annotation or score received (● Nouveau (New) + notification).
    ⏱️ The app also records the time spent on each score: your teacher sees it in their Attendance view, to follow your week's practice.

  5. Your lessons

    📅 Mes cours (My lessons): your upcoming lessons and pending requests. 📅 Voir ses disponibilités (See their availability) shows your teacher's week: their hours, their days off and their Occupé (Busy) slots (you never see the other students). Tap a free slot to propose a lesson, once or every week.

    A lesson is confirmed once you have both accepted it. To move it, propose another time. After each request, the app offers to 📨 prévenir ton prof (let your teacher know) by message (WhatsApp, SMS, email).

    📝 At the end of the lesson, your teacher writes down what to work on: a window opens on your tablet, and the scores concerned are tagged À faire (To do).

Teacher In 6 steps

A computer, an iPad or a tablet is enough. Nothing to install, no account. Each time you open the page, whatever is waiting for your answer (lesson requests, questions) shows up first.

  1. Open the page

    🎼 Open Partoche And Prof

    On iPad: in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen, then always open Partoche And Prof from that icon (your settings are kept safely there).

    Tap the Partoche And Prof logo for Settings: language (FR / EN / 한국어) and browser notifications. The header takes the colour of your emoji.

  2. Your first name, your emoji, your colour

    At the top of My students. They are shown to your students, in a small bubble next to your annotations.

  3. Add your students

    Click the invitation link sent by your student: they are added automatically. Otherwise, My students → Paste a code (the text starting with P1.). Each student has their own tile, with the emoji and colour they chose; tap it to see their scores. Remember to Copy my backup code and keep it in a note: it contains all your students.

  4. Annotate!

    Open a piece: the student's annotations have a bubble with their first name. Switch to Pen and write: each stroke goes to the student right away, and Partoche And Prof checks that it arrived (“Saved at …'s” with the green check). The student sees it live.

    You see your student's tags (New = score sent but not opened yet, To do, In progress, Mastered). A score to give them? ＋ Scores → Send a score to my student, pick the .mscz file on your computer or iPad: it lands in their library.

  5. Follow the week's practice

    The 📊 Attendance button (at the top) shows who is online right now, and for each student the time spent on their scores over the last 7 days. Tap a student: their last connection, their pieces to work on (New, To do, In progress) with the time spent on them, and the day-by-day detail.

  6. Plan the lessons

    📅 My calendar: propose a time to a student (or accept theirs). A lesson is confirmed once you have both accepted it; to move it, either of you can propose another time. On the right of the list: the upcoming lessons. During the lesson a “Lesson in progress” banner appears; at the end, write down what to work on (text + scores): it shows up on the student's side and those scores are tagged To do.

    Repeat: every week or every 2 weeks, for a number of lessons or until a date (e.g. “every Thursday at 10:00 until 5 November”); the student can accept them all at once.

    ⚙️ My availability: your hours (08:00 – 20:00 by default: the last one-hour lesson starts at 19:00), your days off (e.g. Saturday and Sunday) and your unavailable times (a slot or whole days, with a note for yourself). Your students see them as “Busy”, without the other students' names or your notes.

Tips To play well

🔊VolumeTracks → Volume: up to 300 % (above 100 % it may distort), and the tablet volume right below. At the top of the score: ⏱ this session's time and the total on this piece.
📘Student guide (teacher)At the top of the teacher page: the how-to for a new student, sent in one click (WhatsApp, email…).
🎚️TracksShow only your instrument, mute or lower the others.
🔁Loop⟲, tap the start bar, then the end bar, then ▶ Loop.
↔️Continuous lineTablet in landscape: the score scrolls from left to right, with no line breaks.
🎻Bowing⊓ down-bow and V up-bow marks: one tap is enough.
〰️EmojisVibrato, breathe, let ring, tempo… placed with one tap.
🔤Note namesDo Ré Mi or A B C, written under each note.
🐢SpeedFrom 25 to 150 %, to practise slowly.
🎯TunerViolin, viola, cello, guitar, bass, ukulele…
🟢Online“Your teacher is online · on this score”: you know when the other one is there, and on which piece.
⚡LiveThe button for the other person's annotations says “live” or “modified 5 min ago”: you know who is up to date.
🏷️Your tagsTo do, In progress, Mastered… plus your own labels: 🔥 L’enfer (hell), 😎 Easy, 🤭 Marrant (funny), 💪 Déjà bossé (already practised)… in your colours (Options → Mes tags).
💬Ask for adviceLong-press a score → « Demander l’avis de mon prof » (ask my teacher): the teacher answers from the web page (💬 Requests button).
🎹🎵 Change instrumentsTracks → Sound: any instrument for each part, and for piano the right and left hand separately.
🤏Two fingersZoom and scroll, in every layout, even in Pen mode. With a mouse: click and drag.

❓ Questions

The other person's annotations don't show up?

They are tied to the tracks shown and to the note names. Tap the button with the other person's first name: Partoche And Prof offers to switch to their view.

Where is my data?

On your tablet and in your pCloud. There is no Partoche And Prof server, no account, no ads. Only the small “online” signal goes through a public relay (ntfy.sh). Privacy (in French).

Teacher: changing computer or browser?

Your student list is kept in this browser. On the new one, open My students → Paste a code and paste your backup code (or Restore a file).

What about my PDF scores?

Partoche And Prof reads MuseScore files (.mscz). A PDF can be converted with music recognition software (Audiveris, free, or MuseScore's PDF import), but the result often needs corrections. The simplest: look for the piece with LibreScore.

No sound on iPad?

Check that the iPad isn't in silent mode, then press ▶.
